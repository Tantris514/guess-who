# L_Manor — level report (level agent)

`/Game/GW/Maps/L_Manor` — Ashcombe Manor, 1932, at night. One storey plus the hall's gallery (mezzanine).
Everything is generated by scripts and can be rebuilt at any time.

## How to rebuild

| Step | Command (from `Tools/`) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| master materials | `level/run.sh ue_materials.py` | 10 masters in `/Game/GW/Materials/Master` (HLSL checked offline: `python -I -X utf8 level/hlsl_check.py`) |
| arch meshes | `python -I -X utf8 level/gen_arch_meshes.py` then `level/run.sh level/import_meshes.py` | 34 profile/lathe meshes (mouldings, frames, casings, balusters, columns, curtains, chandelier parts) → `/Game/GW/Meshes/Arch`, Nanite shape copies → `/Game/GW/Meshes/Shapes` |
| placeholders | ComfyUI venv python `level/gen_placeholder_textures.py` | procedural textures used until AssetGen ones are approved |
| **refresh everything** | `bash level/update_level.sh` | MIs + texture swap-in from the manifest → rebuild arch/lights/dress/markers/nav/settings → nav check → export → offline validation |
| one part | `level/run.sh build_manor.py arch\|lights\|env\|dress\|markers\|cams\|nav\|settings` | each part deletes only its own `GW_Gen`+`GW_<Part>` actors, then respawns |

`level/run.sh` uses `level/ue_main.py`, a copy of `ue_py.py` that only talks to the interactive editor (when
gameplay runs commandlets they also answer remote execution and `ue_py.py` may pick them).

* **Doors** (`level/validate_offline.py` on `level/instances.json`): every opening and a 1 m approach zone on both sides is free of colliding geometry (13/13 incl. the cloakroom).
* **Z-fighting**: no duplicate instances; coplanar same-facing overlaps of different materials on exposed faces were fixed down to ~50 sub-3 cm² slivers (runner border ends, jamb/threshold corners, book ends).
* **Walkable lanes** (`level/lanes_check.py`: 10 cm raster of all colliding geometry at 0.1–1.5 m, clearance transform): all 13 door openings and every room centre except the two occupied by the dining and billiard tables are connected through lanes ≥ 2 m wide (narrower only within 1.6 m of a door); see `level/lanes.png`. Corridor west/east ends are 5.7 m wide bays with a window (no traps).
* **Shaders**: the headless editor compiles no shaders; the Custom-node HLSL is compiled offline with the engine's dxcompiler (`level/hlsl_check.py`), and the masters were cooked for PCD3D_SM6/SM5 by a targeted cook commandlet (`-cooksinglepackagenorefs`): 0 errors. This includes the fixed `M_GW_Prop`, whose MetalRough sampler now has a matching linear default texture.
